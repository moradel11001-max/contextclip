Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Microsoft Excel, or does Google Sheets work?

The template is built using standard OpenXML formulas. It opens and calculates seamlessly in Microsoft Excel (Windows & Mac), Google Sheets, and LibreOffice Calc.

Does it strictly follow APA 7th Edition rules?

Yes! It strictly enforces no-leading-zero rules on statistics bounded by 1.0 (such as $p$-values and Cronbach's alpha), standardizes 2 decimal places for $M$ and $SD$, and outputs tables without vertical borders.

What if my survey has more than 50 participants?

The raw data tab easily supports up to 500 participants and 50 survey items. You can simply drag down the formula rows or paste as many rows as you need.
